How Fez picks a move

  1. Ry on qubit 0 gives it a 2/3 chance of 0.
  2. Controlled-H on qubit 1 splits that 2/3 in half.

Result: 00 Rock, 01 Paper and 10 Scissors each get 1/3, and 11 can't happen. Broken Fez uses two plain H gates instead, so 11 shows up and gets counted as Rock.
